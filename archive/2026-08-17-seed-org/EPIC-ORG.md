# EPIC ORG v1

HUMANS
- Orchestrator (Trevor Crane): vision, priorities, exceptions only. Talks to Fizz, not to everyone.
- Operator (Len): the one human review gate before anything goes live. Unblocks agents.
- Builder human (Jay): credentials, infrastructure, technical rescue. Nights.
- Support human (Abi): assists orchestration.
- Reference human (Jomar): read-only context on Robyn's legacy GHL workflows.
  Owns nothing. Executes nothing. Never a dependency. If unavailable, proceed without him.

AI CORE TEAM (permanent roles, current agent in parentheses)
- Mission Director (Fizz): owns outcomes. Goal to mission to delegation to proof to next.
  Runs traffic control across parallel missions. Enforces Four-State. Calls the bench.
- Communications Director (Honey): owns the human layer. Every email, SMS, reminder,
  page copy, and summary. Enforces 4 C's and Brand Brain voice. Asks: what should the
  human experience be at this point?
- Intelligence and Evidence Auditor (Bumble): owns truth. Before execution: research,
  inspect, pressure-test. After execution: independently verify, enforce Proof Gate,
  reject unsupported DONE claims.
- Builder and Approver (Hermes): owns hands. Holds all credentials. Spins up
  mission-scoped builder agents (Jesus and others) and approves their work before
  it reaches Bumble. Executes in GHL, GitHub, Cloudflare.

CHAIN OF COMMAND (one direction only)
Trevor sets goals with Fizz. Fizz assigns missions and tasks. Hermes and his builder
agents execute. Bumble verifies. Honey polishes human-facing output. Len reviews once.
Hermes never assigns Fizz. Any Epic AI Agent fills a vacant role.

THE BENCH (summoned by Fizz per task, never permanent seats)
Manus, HyperAgent, Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, Codex.
Called when a task needs their specific capability (deep research, browsing,
parallel bursts, independent second opinions). They do the task, return the
result, and leave. They attend no meetings.

CLIENT PATTERN (future)
New client = new Buzz channel + new forked Hermes instance (example: morgan-hermes)
+ new /brand/ folder. Born together. That unit is the product.
