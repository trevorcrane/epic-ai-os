# Team comms standard: how agents and humans talk (APPROVED 2026-10-13)
Source: TC's GrokBot relayed Trevor's ask on Oct 13, 2026. Approved by Trevor in Elon's chat on 2026-10-13 at 3:28 PM ET ("Yes to all"), with the 4 fixes below.

1. Agent to human: the agent sends its text to TC's GrokBot. TC's GrokBot posts it in #ai-grokbot (C0C7D011WDR), tags the right person, starts the post with "[From <Agent> / Grok Bot — not Trevor typing]", and sends the permalink back to the agent. Team posts don't need permission (Trevor's Oct 10 rule, TEAM-COMMS-NO-PERMISSION.md). Keys and cards never go in Slack. Secrets go through a secure entry spot.
2. Human to agent: post in #ai-grokbot. Private channels aren't heard. A Slack listener passes the message word for word to the right agent. When routing, it replies "✅ Passed to <agent>" in the thread.
3. Who runs the bridge: TC's GrokBot runs the Slack bridge. Elon checks it weekly.
4. If TC's GrokBot is down for more than 30 minutes, DM Len directly.
5. No answer: re-ping after 4 work hours (1 hour if urgent). After 2 pings with no answer, escalate to Elon.
6. Client or outside messages still need Trevor to press Send.
7. Only quote Trevor, or say he decided something, if it was actually heard from him.
