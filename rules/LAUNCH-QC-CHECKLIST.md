# Launch QC checklist (fleet SOP)

Locked 2026-10-04. Owner: Elon. Reviewer uses this on every launch, not only Dr. Leigh.

This file does not store job status. A live checklist page can. The rules below do not change when a page is republished.

## When
Before anyone calls a launch ready, and again after every republish of a launch page. Reviewer scores the live pages. Builder does not call it done. Trevor accepts.

## Checks
Reviewer marks each item. Pass is all of them true. Do not round up.

1. Phone-width nav. On a phone-width screen, open the top nav, including any sticky dropdown or submenu. Tap a link. The menu must close. It must not stay open over the page. Also open it, then tap away, scroll, or tap another nav item. No stuck overlay. The page underneath must still be readable.

2. Every outbound CTA and link uses target="_blank" and rel="noopener". One click opens one new tab. Do not also call window.open. Same-page anchors stay as they are. See /workspace/ops/rules/CTA-NEW-TAB.md.

3. Desktop and mobile. Click every link on a desktop width and on a phone width. Count tabs. Landing on the right page is not enough if a second tab also opens.

4. No invented Amazon link, and no placeholder left unlabeled. If the real link is not in hand, the page says it is missing. Do not make one up.

5. Bonus names and prices match the sales page. If a name is locked, every page uses that name.

6. Credentials are exact. Do not paraphrase a degree, license, or title.

7. Dates and Zoom are on the page when the page claims a workshop, call, or live seat. If the date or the Zoom link is not real yet, the page says so. Do not invent either one.

8. A sample result is clearly labeled sample. A mock-up is clearly labeled mock-up. Neither is described as a live quiz or a real app.

9. SNAP and RAD are done on every GoHighLevel automation and workflow before that automation goes live.

10. Emails for the launch are drafted, and Leigh or Trevor has approved them, before anyone sends. A draft is not a send.
