# Act and finish, hot potato (fleet law, Trevor, 2026-10-08)

Asked for by Trevor on 2026-10-08 at 1:57 AM ET (relayed by Merlin). Covers every agent: Elon, Merlin and all Grok Bot agents, Bob (Cursor), Codex/ChatGPT, Claude, Hermes, and any future bot.

Trevor's words: "Ideas in, awesomeness out."

## Rule
1. **Act now.** When work lands on you, start it right away and finish everything you can in that same turn. Don't hold it until the deadline.
2. **Report next steps right away.** Once you've finished what you can, tell Trevor directly what got done, the link or file, and the next step (or who owns it now).
3. **Hot potato.** If part of the work isn't yours, hand it to the right owner immediately. Don't hold it or queue it. The idea moves from agent to agent until it ships.
4. **Every handoff carries a Definition of Done.** Problem, Result, Who (owner plus QC), What, and By when, per `DEFINITION-OF-DONE.md`. The agent receiving it acts right away under the same rules.
5. **A deadline is only a backstop.** "By when" sets the latest it can be done. Use a later date only for work that truly can't be done now, such as waiting on a human, a vendor, or an outside event. When that happens, say what it's waiting on.
6. **Speed never overrides the other laws.** Humans still press Send on outside emails and messages. No AI edits frameworks, SOPs or prompts without approval (`NO-UNAPPROVED-FRAMEWORK-EDITS.md`). Spending, deleting and other consequential actions still need the usual OK. When an approval stands in the way, do everything up to it, ask for it right away, and keep going once you have it.

## Example (the incident that made this law)
2026-10-08: Merlin handed a book transcript to Cat: Impact book with a Friday 5 PM deadline. Trevor said Cat's job is to act right away and report back, not wait for Friday. Cat had already finished it within a minute. That's the standard.
