**What it is, in his words.** "We're all on the same business. It's the love business." [ECR, VERIFIED] The Brain doc: "Trevor believes we're in the same business, and that is the most successful business in the history of the world. Make things you love for people you love. Only work with people who love, who love what they do, and every day you get to live in love." [BB PDF, Rule 8, BELIEVED 85%] It runs on three questions, his own: "what do you love ... who do you love ... what do they love? Being problems solved and results delivered." **How he sells with it.** "I define selling as making desirable." "I don't sell. I love. And love, done right, is the most powerful sales tool in existence."
