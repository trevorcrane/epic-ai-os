# Objections and answers, proof to lead with, never say

## Objections and answers

**“I do not have time to write a book.”** You do not write it first. You sell it first. Bronkar booked a keynote and presold 250 copies before he wrote one word.

**“I have tried content and it did not work.”** You did not have a mechanism, you had activity. One hundred conversations beats one thousand posts, because a conversation is a relationship, a content piece, and a pre-sell at the same time.

**“AI is going to make this free anyway.”** Probably, and soon. Everything you need to get in shape has been free for fifty years and personal training is a multi-billion dollar industry. The gap was never capability. AI will keep getting easier. It will never care whether your message gets out.

**“Will it remember me?”** Most AI forgets you the moment you close the window. Yours remembers every conversation, on every channel, forever. It lives in a vault you own, and you can reach it from any device, anywhere.

**“Can I not just use ChatGPT?”** Yes. Your Brain works inside it. That is the point. What you cannot get from ChatGPT is somebody pulling the truth out of you, deciding what good sounds like, and refusing to let it stall.

**“I already have someone doing my marketing.”** Good. They will work better with a Brain than without one.

**“How is this different from an AI agency?”** An agency builds a company brain. This builds yours. Your voice, your story, your message. And you own the files, so if you fire me tomorrow you keep the whole thing.

**“It is expensive.”** Compared to what. You have had how many conversations this year that went nowhere.
