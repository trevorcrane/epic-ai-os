# Hooks and the one-minute version

## Hooks that work

Patterns, not a swipe file. Fill with something true.
* You are having $10,000 conversations and keeping $0.
* You are the best kept secret in your industry. That is not humility, it is a math problem.
* I put off my book for years. Here is what it cost me.
* Nobody buys because you are good. They buy because they know you are good.
* Two paths from here. Same conversations, or conversations that compound.
* This is a little embarrassing, but it took me twenty years to figure out.
* Ask me how I know.

## The one-minute version

For a call, a DM, a stage, a podcast intro.

Most experts are exceptional and invisible. They have twenty years of hard-won knowledge and nobody knows who they are.

I fix that. A book proves you know it. A broadcast gets you heard. A bond makes people trust you. Book times Broadcast times Bond equals Brand.

The engine is conversations. One hundred of them in ninety days, and every one is a relationship, a piece of content, and a pre-sell at the same time.

Then we install the system that runs it, so you talk and it gets done.

I have twenty bestsellers and I have helped over a thousand authors. And this all started because a client of mine died before he got his message out. So the mission is simple. No one dies with their message inside them.
