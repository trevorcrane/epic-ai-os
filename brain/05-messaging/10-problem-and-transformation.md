# Persuasion spine, the problem, the transformation

## The persuasion spine

Default order for anything that has to persuade:

**PROMISE → PROOF → PROBLEM → PATH**

Variations that work: - Promise → Problem → Proof → Process - Promise → Proof → Problem → Story → Process - Hook → Proof → “not what you think” → Problem → self-deprecating story → turning point → framework → result → next move

The rule underneath it: **if there is no promise and no proof, nobody trusts the process.** Process only matters after attention and trust are earned.

## The problem, in their words

Never in marketer language. This is what they actually say.
* I do not know what to say.
* I cannot explain why I am different.
* My content is inconsistent and I hate most of it.
* I have the knowledge but nobody knows who I am.
* Everything is trapped in my head and my old calls.
* I have more ideas than I can execute.
* I have tools. I do not have execution.
* I am doing all of it myself.
* I have a book and no business around it.
* I have an audience and no path from attention to revenue.

The 3am version: family, faith, gratitude, whether they are reaching enough people, whether they are known for the right thing, and whether they are building a business that is wrecking the life it was supposed to support.

## The transformation

| From | To |
| :- | :- |
| Invisible | In demand |
| Idea rich, execution poor | Talk it, done |
| Inconsistent content | A media machine |
| Doing everything personally | A team that runs it |
| A book with no system | Authority with a business around it |
| Attention with no path | Reach, relationships, revenue |

Note: "Invisible → In demand" is his approved promise. Whether the public site uses "in demand": Q4.
