# The 4 C's of Communication

**What it is, in his words.** "You can do it in just a few words. It works with your son, your spouse, your client, your team. Miss one and you blow it. Use all four and you're unstoppable." [4Cs, VERIFIED] Connect, "Do I have your attention?" three seconds. Care, "Do you feel heard?" five seconds. Clarity, "Do you know why we're here?" ten seconds. Call to Action, "Do you know what happens next?" five seconds. "Total time: under 30 seconds." [4Cs, VERIFIED] Team version: "We do not assume. We verify." "We guide. We don't chase. We don't absorb every problem." "We are not on-demand problem absorbers. We are strategic guides." [2026 msg, VERIFIED]

**The story he teaches it with.** Maverick. "Maverick, hey brother, come here." Connect. "I love you man." Care. "Your room is a mess and we need to fix that." Clarity. "Go clean it up right now and come get me when you're done." Call to Action. "Four sentences. 15 seconds. Every human being involved knew exactly what was happening and why." [4Cs, VERIFIED] The client version is Joel to Sally: "Hi Sally. This is Joel with Epic Author. I got your message." then "Did I get that right?" then a link or office hours. [ECR, VERIFIED, his own example] What breaks: skip Care and they feel "I'm a transaction." Skip CTA and they think "Okay... so what do I do now?" [4Cs, VERIFIED]

**How he sells with it.** It is the rule for every DM and every client reply. Two CTA types: "Type A, Resource or Content. Send them somewhere." "Type B, Conversation or Action. Get them to do something." With leads the CTA is a conversation. With clients it is content or a call. [4Cs and 2026 msg, VERIFIED]

**What it is NOT.** "This isn't a customer service script." [4Cs, VERIFIED] Not small talk. Not three options: "One. Not three options. Not a list." Not what you will do: "The CTA always belongs to them." [4Cs, VERIFIED] Not a stack of exclamation points: "Hey!!!! Hope you're well!!!! LMK!!!!" is the failure case. [4Cs, VERIFIED]

**Signature lines.** "Connect. Care. Clarity. Call to Action. Every time. With every human. No exceptions." "Clarity is a gift." "One action. Make it easy. Make it clear. Make it theirs." [4Cs, VERIFIED] "Not because you're the smartest. Because you make people feel seen." [4Cs, VERIFIED]
