# Book × Broadcast × Bond = Brand

**What it is, in his words.** "A book that proves you know, a broadcast that gets you heard, a bond that makes people trust you. Multiply, not add." [03-messaging, VERIFIED: v0.2] The Brain doc says it longer: "The Book establishes expertise. The Broadcast distributes it. The Bond is the relationship that converts followers to clients." [BB PDF, BELIEVED 85%: Claude-built from his docs] The multiply rule is the whole point. "One without the others is weak. All three together is a brand that compounds." [TLDR, BELIEVED 80%] A zero anywhere is a zero brand.

**The story he teaches it with.** The book is never the product. "It's not the book that gets you clients. It's the position the book puts you in." [PBYP, BELIEVED 85%] He borrows Ryan Stewman's line and repeats it everywhere: "Idiots don't write books." [BigMoney, VERIFIED, quoting Stewman] Then the book gets amplified: "I don't help people get media. I help them become the media." [BB PDF, Addition 4, BELIEVED 80%] Bronkar is the proof case: a keynote booked and 250 copies presold before a word was written, then a #1 bestseller six weeks later. [WriteRIGHT, VERIFIED]

**How he sells with it.** It is the Epic Media four things on his LinkedIn: "Books, Profit before you publish. Podcasts, Expand your reach and your network. Personal Brand, Your story. Your voice. AI Systems, Clone your best thinking." [LI About, VERIFIED] Book is Books. Broadcast is Podcasts plus Personal Brand. Bond is the conversations. On a call he asks which factor is the zero.

**What it is NOT.** Not a publishing pitch. "Your book isn't your business. Your book positions you to build your business." [PBYP, BELIEVED 85%] Not three separate services you can buy one of. Not "get media." Become it.

**Signature lines.** "The book can be your most powerful marketing tool." [BB PDF, VERIFIED] "The book is the business card." "Don't get media. Become it." [BB PDF, BELIEVED 80%] "Your book is not your product. It is BAIT to attract your ideal customer." [BigMoney, VERIFIED]
