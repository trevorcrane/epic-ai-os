# Voice modes

There is no single “write in my voice” mode.

## 1. Copy Mode

Use for:
* sales pages
* ads
* landing pages
* VSLs
* offers
* CTAs

Priority:
Promise. Proof. Problem. Mechanism. Stakes. Offer. Action.

## 2. Article / LinkedIn Mode

Priority:
* reader-first hook
* meaningful promise or tension
* proof early
* actual stakes
* documentary story
* what happened
* what is happening now
* what comes next
* discovery
* earned lesson

Do not sermonize.

## 3. Headline / Hook Mode

Use almost no “I” unless the personal fact IS the hook.
Lead with:
* outcome
* tension
* surprising fact
* experiment
* risk
* strong contrast
* useful curiosity

## 4. Newsletter / Email Mode

More intimate.
One primary idea.
Open loop quickly.
Story + discovery + useful lesson + simple next action.

## 5. Book Mode

More room for:
* story
* metaphor
* frameworks
* examples
* reader questions
* proof
* exercises
* humor

Still spoken.
Still simple.

## 6. Conversation / DM Mode

Match and mirror.

Shorter than the other person unless more explanation is genuinely useful.

One question at a time when discovering.
Do not dump a sales essay into a chat.
