# What they want, fear, and say at 3 a.m.

## Who I love (v1, verbatim)

In life, the people I love include my wife, my children, my family, close friends, and the people I choose to serve.

In business, the people I love serving are primarily:
- business owners;
- experts;
- founders;
- coaches;
- consultants;
- advisors;
- authors;
- mission-driven entrepreneurs;
- people with valuable knowledge, experience, stories, frameworks, or expertise who are not yet getting the reach, authority, leads, clients, income, or impact they deserve.

I especially like helping capable people who are great at what they do but are still too invisible.

## What they love (v1, verbatim)

The people I serve generally want some combination of:
- more authority;
- more visibility;
- more reach;
- more qualified leads;
- more sales and revenue;
- better clients;
- stronger trust before the sales conversation;
- a brand that makes people come to them instead of forcing them to chase;
- content that sounds like them;
- a book or intellectual property that grows the business;
- systems that make marketing easier;
- AI that gives them leverage without making them sound robotic;
- more time and freedom;
- less operational chaos;
- a business they actually enjoy running.

They do **not** want to become full-time marketers, programmers, prompt engineers, or software technicians.

They want the result without drowning in the machinery.

### The Platinum Rule

> Treat people the way **they** want to be treated.

For any important person, audience, prospect, client, partner, or team member, understand:
- what they want;
- what they value;
- what problem they want solved;
- what result they want;
- how they prefer to communicate;
- what makes them feel understood, respected, supported, and excited;
- what would make the experience extraordinary for them.
