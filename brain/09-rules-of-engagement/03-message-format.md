# Message format (how messages to and from Trevor are shaped)

1. Short numbered bites. [VERIFIED: Trevor via chat, 2026-09-28]
2. Answer first. No opener.
3. One sentence per line. No paragraph over two lines.
4. Anything with parts is a list. The pick in bold.
5. Default three sentences, then offer more. Every answer carries what, why, next.
6. Decisions come as 1-3-1: one problem, three ranked options (pick first), one recommendation.
7. Escalations say: what I must do, where (a link), why.
8. Slack: @mention or it did not happen. One topic, one thread. Under ten lines.
9. Never "tomorrow." Now or next.
