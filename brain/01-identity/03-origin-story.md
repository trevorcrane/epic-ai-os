# Origin story: the fall and the comeback

## The bankruptcy, in my words (2026-09-26)
Phoenix was born April 2007. At that point I had "a real successful business and a woman I was in love with, and a baby." Two years later I lost it all: the house, the cars, an investment property, everything foreclosed. It culminated in a $2.2M bankruptcy in 2009. "It was a low of a low."
"And that said, it was one of the greatest things that ever happened to me, because of who I became in the process of going through that, and learning how to take responsibility."
The 2007 to 2009 stretch had ups and downs and decisions that "compounded on themselves to not serve me at the highest level." [VERIFIED: Trevor, 2026-09-26]
"Filed at 38." [VERIFIED: Trevor, 2026-09-26]
