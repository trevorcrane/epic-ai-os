# Progressive Phone Opt-In
Reusable website feature | Approved pattern requested by Trevor, September 24, 2026.

## Request phrase
“Use our Progressive Phone Opt-In.”

## Specification
Match the website's fonts, colors, spacing and button style. Open the signup form in a popup when the main CTA is clicked. Initially show only “Your First Name” and “Your Best Email.” Once a name and valid email are entered, reveal “Your Cell (optional)” inline in the same popup, inside a subtly shaded section. Do not open a second popup. Keep phone optional and allow signup without it.

Below the phone field, show a short bold invitation and one sentence explaining the specific benefit of sharing a number. Adapt both to the offer. Example:

**Join my VIP text community**
Book holders get 1st access to freebies, bonuses & exclusive offers.

Keep one clear submit button. If the offer is genuinely free, center “100% Free” below it; otherwise omit that line. Preserve the CRM/workflow connection, accessible labels, mobile usability and any applicable SMS consent/opt-out requirements. Providing a phone number alone must not silently enable marketing texts. Test the reveal, phone-free submission, CRM capture, redirect and access-message delivery.

For an inline form instead of a popup, request: “Inline Progressive Phone Opt-In.” The same reveal behavior applies.

## Application scope
Use this pattern when requested for the job. Do not automatically retrofit unrelated forms. Review under EPIC-REVIEWER-POLICY.md and record the implementation version and end-to-end evidence.
