# Trevor’s exact critique prompt
Source: direct owner message, September 24, 2026. Quoted wording retained.

> Score that from 1-100. Tell me: — What score you’d give it and why — The single biggest thing holding it back from a higher score — One specific rewrite that would move it at least 10 points higher
>
> I want you to score this in regards to the most effective, the most efficient. The most clear. Consider it's likelihood of getting me the best result the fastest. If it's copy, the most compelling and likely to influence someone to do the thing we want them to do.
>
> Then show me the rewrite. After the rewrite, score it again. Keep doing this until it hits 95 or above. Don’t ask me anything. Just improve and rescore until it gets there.

Interpretation: iterate autonomously on the requested draft. Do not manufacture a ten-point gain, claim certainty about conversion, or treat the score as permission to publish. Above 90, a ten-point gain may be mathematically impossible; pursue the largest justified improvement within the 100-point scale. Preserve the original instruction rather than silently rewriting it.
