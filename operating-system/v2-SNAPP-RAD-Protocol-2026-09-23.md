# SNAPP RAD protocol

Version: 2 | Date: 2026-09-23 | Status: source-grounded draft for review.
Spelling: SNAPP RAD, with two P letters.

## Meaning and evidence

| Letter | Meaning | What the job must specify and verify |
|---|---|---|
| S | Site / Sites | Exact websites, pages, funnels, URLs, access conditions, and the visitor journey. Check each intended action at the actual destination. |
| N | Notifications | Internal team alerts when action is taken or required. Identify recipient, trigger, channel, actionable content, and a test receipt. |
| A | Automations | Client/prospect emails, SMS, messages, and workflows. Identify entry, timing/timezone, audience, personalization, suppression, exits, replies, and delivery tests. |
| P | Pipeline | Lead/client stages in Smart Marketing AI, transition triggers, accountable owner, tracking, and reporting. Verify the test contact moves correctly. |
| P | Protocol | Instructions, scripts, SOPs, roles, steps, dependencies, links, and recovery instructions so people and agents know what to do. |
| R | Reviewed | Separate reviewer inspects the actual version and records requirements checked, results, evidence, and unresolved issues. |
| A | Approved | Named authority approves the stated version and action, or standing authorization is cited. Copy approval, deployment authority, and broadcast authority remain distinct. |
| D | Delivered | The intended authorized result is live, working, and accessible at its destination, supported by observed evidence. Draft-file delivery alone is not a live-funnel delivery claim. |

Notifications are internal. Automations are customer/prospect facing. CRM Pipeline stages describe a person or account's journey; Linear statuses describe the team's project work.

RAD is an evidence gate. For an overall deliverable, report “Delivered, awaiting Trevor acceptance” when all three have evidence. Internal steps may hand off on verified evidence without waiting for Trevor. Only Trevor declares his project done. This overrides older descriptions equating RAD with agent-declared completion.

## Per-job worksheet

- Job ID, brand/tenant, department, project title, business outcome.
- One accountable PM, builder, separate reviewer, and designated approver.
- Due date with timezone, next checkpoint, dependencies, scope, and existing authority.
- Testable acceptance criteria and exact source/artifact version.
- For each of S, N, A, P, P: resource IDs/URLs, owner, requirements, test, result, evidence link, gaps, and next action.
- Review verdict and inspected version; approval scope and supporting record; delivery destination and observed result/time.
- Trevor's acceptance record when supplied. Until then keep acceptance pending.

## Execution sequence

1. Inspect existing assets and scope the job. Verify current cohort, offer, URLs, recipients, and tenant. Do not copy dates or IDs from an older campaign.
2. Build under existing authority. Name one editor per shared resource. Preserve a recovery route for live changes.
3. Review the whole journey with designated test contacts: entry, duplicates, missing data, links, notifications, messages, pipeline transitions, suppression, and access. Tailor cases to the actual job.
4. Fix failed checks and recheck affected behavior. Reviewer records evidence against the revised version.
5. Use the applicable approval record. Deploy only the authorized scope, then verify at the actual destination.
6. Return a versioned report in the originating thread and link it to the tracked job. Report gaps candidly. Keep the parent deliverable awaiting Trevor acceptance while continuing dependent and unrelated authorized work. Internal task progress follows the charter lifecycle.
