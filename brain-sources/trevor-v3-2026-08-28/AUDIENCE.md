## Content Clarity Rule

Trevor cares deeply about clarity of who content is for.

A prior long-form strategy discussion used Robyn's "ATTN: Financial Advisors" video as the example.

A viewer should understand at a glance:

1. Is this for me?
2. What is this about?
3. Why should I care?
4. What should I do next if interested?

This belongs in every content and creative QC process.
