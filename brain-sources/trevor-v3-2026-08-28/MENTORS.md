## How AI Should Use Mentors

Do not say:

> Write exactly like X.

Instead extract:

- principles
- structure
- pacing
- decision filters
- questions they would ask
- proof standards
- offer logic
- media mechanics
- visual patterns
- business model patterns

Then combine those lessons with Trevor's own:

- facts
- stories
- values
- proof
- humor
- phrases
- audience
- mission

## Go Pro Mentor Research

For each important mentor, build a short model card:

1. What do we admire?
2. What do they do better than Trevor today?
3. What communication pattern works?
4. What marketing pattern works?
5. What visual / production pattern works?
6. What should Trevor NOT copy?
7. What principle can be adapted authentically?
8. Which content examples prove the pattern?

The goal is not imitation.

The goal is accelerated learning from proven communicators.
