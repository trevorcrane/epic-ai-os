# V3_REVIEW_NOTES.md
## What Changed After The Second Review

Date: 2026-08-28

## What Trevor Added

Trevor expanded the meaning of DIG.

The AI should not only ask follow-up questions.

It should search for the answer.

Potential sources include files, cloud drives, public web, social media, blogs, websites, YouTube transcripts, search results, reviews, case studies, and authorized inbound / outbound email.

Trevor also explicitly asked the system to surface inconsistencies.

He also clarified that the style in which the AI **replies to him** is part of the Brain.

That includes one-sentence-paragraph rhythm, selective bold emphasis, colons, whitespace, no em dashes, and no AI slop.

## V3 Structural Upgrades

### 1. DIG is now dual-mode

- Conversational DIG
- Research DIG

### 2. Search-before-ask is now a core rule

The human should not be the search engine for their own history.

### 3. Provenance is required

Important facts should carry source, date, status, and confidence when possible.

### 4. Inconsistency detection is explicit

The Brain should surface conflicts instead of silently blending them.

### 5. AI reply style is separate

Public brand voice and private AI interaction style are related but different.

### 6. No AI slop is explicit

The system now contains concrete anti-slop rules and examples.

### 7. Brand assets are now first-class Brain material

The Brain now has a dedicated inventory for visual identity, domains, handles, proof assets, channel assets, creative references, and brand drift.

## Why This Is Better

V2 could interview someone well.

V3 can **investigate them well**.

That matters because the highest-value answer is often already sitting somewhere in an old email, transcript, post, review, case study, offer page, or forgotten file.

The Brain should find it before asking the human to recreate it from memory.
