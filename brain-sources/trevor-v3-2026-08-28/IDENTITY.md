### Steven

Trevor's live 2026 intake described a client named Steven whose dreams, family, and mission Trevor cared about deeply. Steven began withdrawing, later asked for a partial refund, and Trevor refused because he believed holding Steven to the dream Steven said he wanted was the more honest and loving choice. Steven later died by suicide.

A source found in Google Drive, titled **Suicide Power Story**, adds important primary-source detail. It was posted 2018-02-25 and identifies the client as **Steven Woloszyk**. Trevor wrote that Steven was a previous client, had quit before writing and publishing his book, and died by suicide.

A 2020 entry in that same source contains language that closely matches Trevor's current mission:

> I'm not enough.
>
> It takes a village.
>
> ...what people need... is to hear YOUR VOICE.

It continues with the belief that Trevor is here to help people share their voice, message, and story, because one person's voice may be exactly what another person needs to hear.

That source is now a PRIORITY origin-story asset.

Important truth rule:

Do not oversimplify suicide into one cause.

Do not claim Trevor could have prevented Steven's death.

Trevor's meaning is about responsibility to contribute, ask for help, share voices, and stop playing small.

## AI Caveman

[LOCKED AS A POINT OF VIEW, NOT THE MASTER BRAND]

Trevor is developing an "AI Caveman" way of thinking.

The point is not that AI is dumb.

The point is that Trevor does not want to become a computer scientist to use it.

Desired experience:

> Me want thing.
>
> Me explain thing.
>
> Machine help build thing.

The serious thesis underneath the joke:

AI should reduce execution friction.

Trevor does not want another tool that gives advice and wishes him luck.

He wants tools, technology, and an AI team that execute.
