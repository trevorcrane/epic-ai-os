# DIGGING_FOR_GOLD.md
## The EPIC Brain Research + Discovery Protocol

Status: V1, created for EPIC Brain V3.

## The Big Idea

Gold is not only in the person's head.

Gold is everywhere they have left evidence of who they are, what they believe, what they sell, what worked, what failed, what people say about them, and how they actually communicate.

So DIG has two engines:

**CONVERSATIONAL DIG**

Ask better questions.

**RESEARCH DIG**

Search better sources.

The AI should use both.

## The Default Rule

**Search before asking.**

Before asking a human to remember something, check whether the answer already exists.

Before accepting a vague claim, check whether proof exists.

Before locking a decision, check whether a newer decision contradicts it.

Before saying "we don't know," check the obvious sources.

## The DIG Ladder

Use the cheapest, fastest, highest-signal source first.

### Level 1: Brain + Current Context

Search:

- core Brain files
- decision log
- gaps file
- current conversation
- uploaded files

### Level 2: Private / Owned Sources

When connected and authorized, search:

- Google Drive
- Dropbox
- OneDrive or other storage
- inbound email
- sent email
- CRM notes and messages
- Slack / Teams / internal chat
- call recordings and transcripts
- meeting notes
- client onboarding
- case-study folders
- testimonial folders
- support conversations
- contracts / proposals / scope docs when relevant
- analytics exports
- ad reports

### Level 3: Owned Public Sources

Search:

- company website
- personal website
- blog archive
- public social profiles
- newsletters / public archives
- YouTube channel
- YouTube transcripts
- podcast feeds
- public talks / interviews
- press pages
- book pages
- author pages

### Level 4: External Public Evidence

Search:

- search engines
- public social mentions
- interviews on other people's channels
- podcast appearances
- press mentions
- public reviews
- Amazon book reviews when relevant
- customer / client public comments
- event pages
- public case studies
- partner websites
- backlinks / references

### Level 5: Mentor / Market Research

Search public work from:

- mentors
- category leaders
- competitors
- adjacent brands
- creators the person wants to model

Extract principles and patterns.

Do not copy identity.

## Evidence Card

For every high-value discovery, capture:

**FINDING:**

What did we learn?

**SOURCE:**

Where did it come from?

**DATE:**

When was the source created or last updated?

**TYPE:**

Raw quote / fact / claim / proof / opinion / AI inference.

**STATUS:**

LOCKED / WORKING / HISTORICAL / VERIFY / NEED MORE / CONFLICT.

**CONFIDENCE:**

High / Medium / Low.

**DESTINATION:**

Which Brain file should be updated?

## Conversational Gold Loop

When research cannot finish the answer, return to the human.

Use:

> Tell me more about that.

Then:

> Can you give me an example?

Alternate naturally.

Then reflect:

> It sounds like...

Then confirm:

> Close?

Capture:

- RAW GOLD
- DISTILLED GOLD
- STATUS
- SOURCE

## Research Gold Loop

1. Define the missing question.
2. Search internal sources.
3. Search private connected sources when authorized.
4. Search public sources when useful.
5. Collect evidence.
6. Compare dates and authority.
7. Surface conflicts.
8. Draft the likely truth.
9. Ask the human only to resolve what remains.
10. Update the Brain and decision log.

## Inconsistency Mining

Do not treat inconsistencies as annoying cleanup.

They are gold.

They show where identity, brand, offer, proof, or strategy is changing.

Look for:

- different bios
- different titles
- different prices
- different audience definitions
- different promises
- different revenue numbers
- different bestseller counts
- different offer names
- different program lengths
- old links still live
- old content that contradicts current strategy
- public claims unsupported by internal evidence
- internal beliefs not reflected in public brand
- stated goals contradicted by actual activity

When found, log them in `CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md`.

## Permission + Privacy Rule

Search only sources the AI is authorized and technically able to access.

Do not bypass access controls.

Do not pretend to have scraped or searched something that was not actually accessed.

Respect platform rules and legal / contractual restrictions.

For sensitive private data, use the minimum necessary information.

## The Outcome

A strong DIG process should reduce the number of questions the human has to answer.

It should increase:

- accuracy
- specificity
- proof
- authenticity
- clarity
- consistency
- useful surprise

The person should occasionally think:

> Holy shit. You found that?

That is the standard.
