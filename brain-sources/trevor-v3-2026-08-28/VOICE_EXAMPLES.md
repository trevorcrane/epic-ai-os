## APPROVED TEST: Bestseller Article Pattern

Trevor called the latest revised bestseller sample "golden."

Important characteristics from that test:

- starts with a simple promise
- establishes proof quickly
- softens proof with self-deprecating humor
- uses "This is a little embarrassing..."
- tells the 20-year struggle
- introduces mentor / discovery
- admits he did not even know the questions to ask
- uses short sentences
- creates a turning point
- shifts from "getting my book done" as finish line to promoting first
- shows 10X result
- teaches after the story earns the lesson

## Exact Tone Correction That Matters

Rejected:

> So yeah, I know a little bit about this shit.

Why:

It made Trevor sound like a dick.

Preferred:

> So... I do know a little bit about this sh... stuff. lol

Why:

Same confidence.

Less chest-beating.

More playful.

More Trevor.

## Self-Deprecation Example

Preferred setup:

> This is a little embarrassing...
>
> But it took me more than 20 years to finally write my first book.

This is much better than turning the fact into heroic mythology.

## Long-Form Structure Already In Trevor's Books

Trevor's books repeatedly use:

- big promise
- big problem
- proof / case study
- personal mistake
- mentor / new idea
- simple framework
- specific action

That means the modern voice should tighten and update the pattern, not erase it.

## Current Writing Formula

For persuasive educational content:

**PROMISE -> PROOF -> PROBLEM -> PERSONAL / SELF-DEPRECATING STORY -> DISCOVERY -> PATH / PLAN / PROTOCOL -> RESULT -> NEXT MOVE**

Not every piece needs every step.

But most strong pieces need:

- a reason to care
- a reason to believe
- a problem or tension
- a discovery
- a useful next step

## Documentary Formula

Trevor's documentary content should answer:

- What happened?
- What is happening right now?
- What are we testing?
- What is at stake?
- What do we know?
- What do we not know?
- What happens next?

Then lead the reader toward the lesson.

Do not write:

> Here are the 5 lessons you must learn from my journey.

when the story can make the reader discover the lesson naturally.

## Reader-First Hook Rule

Bad default:

> I'm building an AI team...

Stronger direction:

> What if a $1M+ business could grow without adding a giant team?

Then bring Trevor / Robyn into the experiment.

Use "I" when the personal fact is proof or story.

Not because the author is automatically the main character.

## Voice Test Protocol

When improving voice:

1. Give the AI a real task.
2. Let it write.
3. Ask the person what feels wrong.
4. Capture corrections literally.
5. Rewrite once.
6. Save both the rule and the example.

Questions:

- What sentence sounds least like you?
- What word would you never say?
- Where do you sound like a dick?
- Where is it too polished?
- Where is it boring?
- Where is the humor missing?
- Where did the AI make you sound smarter than you would talk?
- Where did it lose the stakes?

The correction session is voice training.
