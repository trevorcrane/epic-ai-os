# CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md
## EPIC Brain Conflict + Drift Monitor

Status: V1.

## Purpose

The AI should not merely collect information.

It should notice when the information disagrees.

That includes contradictions, stale material, unsupported claims, and brand drift.

## Conflict Types

### 1. Decision Conflict

Two different decisions exist.

Example:

- old audience = authors
- current audience = broader experts / founders

### 2. Offer Conflict

Different prices, names, tiers, payment plans, or program lengths exist.

### 3. Proof Conflict

A result is quoted differently in different places.

### 4. Identity Conflict

Bio, title, mission, positioning, or company description changed.

### 5. Public vs Private Conflict

Website / social copy says one thing while the current internal decision says another.

### 6. Strategy vs Behavior Conflict

The stated priority is not reflected in execution.

### 7. Brand Voice Conflict

Approved voice says one thing but actual content repeatedly sounds different.

### 8. Brand Asset Conflict

Old logos, colors, domains, bios, thumbnails, URLs, or sub-brand names remain in circulation.

## Audit Cadence

Run a consistency audit:

- before a major site rewrite
- before a new offer launch
- before a bio / speaker kit update
- before training an autonomous marketing agent
- after a major strategic pivot
- quarterly for the full Brain

## Rule

**Never let stale certainty beat current truth.**
