# Handoff rules

Trevor Epic Team v0.2 draft, 2026-10-06. Updated by Elon from the v0.1 draft (2026-09-28).
Status: WORKING · private. Not approved by Trevor.
Sources: repo/team/FIRST-MATE.md, LEAD.md, TALK.md, Trevor chat 2026-09-28, rules/TANGO-HANDOFF-NO-DROP.md, rules/CURSOR-BOB-DISPATCH.md

1. Work moves between houses on the Tango task, never through chat. [VERIFIED: LEAD.md]
2. The handoff note is five lines, on the task: **result wanted · decided · tried · open · where the files are.** [VERIFIED: FIRST-MATE.md]
3. Assign the task to the other house's Lead. Tango notifies them. The sender stays on the hook until they confirm. [VERIFIED: FIRST-MATE.md]
4. No Tango in this harness? Post the task text in the thread, prefixed TASK:, and @mention a Lead that has Tango. That Lead owns it. [VERIFIED: FIRST-MATE.md]
5. Twice with no answer: tell the Captain in one line and offer the fastest path. [VERIFIED: FIRST-MATE.md]
6. Agents coordinate through the task, not bot-to-bot chatter. Only the Lead posts job events. [VERIFIED: TALK.md]
7. Handoffs are event-driven: a handoff happens because an event fired (`team/05-handoffs/04-events.md`), not because someone polled or pinged. [VERIFIED: Trevor via chat, 2026-09-28]
8. A handoff is never to the Captain. The Captain gets results, 1-3-1s, and BLOCKED, nothing to manage. [VERIFIED: FIRST-MATE.md]
9. A deliverable counts only as a Tango artifact on a named, assigned task. The receiver posts a readback (version, heading, source count) before it is marked delivered. [rules/TANGO-HANDOFF-NO-DROP.md]
10. Code and repo changes go to Bob, titled "Bob: ...", pull requests only. Not through Tango. [rules/CURSOR-BOB-DISPATCH.md]
11. Bob cannot see the box. Bots without Tango (Bob, and Codex outside its worker) get their context inside the prompt; ChatGPT or Elon carries Tango updates for them. [rules/CURSOR-BOB-DISPATCH.md; rules/TANGO-HANDOFF-NO-DROP.md for Codex]
